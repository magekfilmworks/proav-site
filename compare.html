Show call — rendered vs live

The same show, both panes, started on the same press. Left is the encoded file the site now serves. Right is the live DOM stage running in an iframe, which is what shipped before it. Only one is audible at a time, because two callers at once tells you nothing.

—

What to look at: type edges on the cue lines, the two space clips at 0:17 and 2:43, and whether the GOs land on the same word. What is not comparable: the live stage re-rasterises text at whatever size the pane is, so it will always look sharper than a fixed encode in a small box — judge the file at the size it will actually be played, or full screen. Drift: the two can separate by a few frames over four minutes. They were started together, not locked together.
