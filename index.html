THE SHOW

Where live production actually happens.

ProAV goes where the show actually happens — Mainstage, Backstage, and increasingly, Cloud. Each episode pairs a hands-on look at the gear and workflows driving live production with real conversations from the people behind them: FOH engineers, LDs, video directors, production managers, and the hardware companies building the tools that make it all work — plus the teams rethinking what "live" means as more of it moves to remote and cloud-based workflows. Some episodes run the full demo-plus-conversation format with a single guest; others go deep with multiple guests for a full hour. Available as audio and video, wherever you listen or watch.
