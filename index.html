THE SHOW

Where live production actually happens.

Each episode pairs a hands-on look at the gear and workflows driving live production with FOH engineers, LDs, video directors, production managers, and the hardware companies building the tools that make it all work. Some episodes run the full demo-plus-conversation format with a single guest; others go deep with multiple guests for a full hour.

MAINSTAGE

The gear driving the show.

A focused demo of the gear behind the episode — not a full rig walkthrough, just what's actually driving the show.

BACKSTAGE

The people behind it.

The conversation — career paths, hard calls, and what it actually took to pull the show off.

CLOUD

Where it's all headed.

Remote production, AV-over-IP, and the teams rethinking what "live" means when it's cloud-native.

WHO'S ON THE SHOW

Built by the people who run it.

AV
AV Companies

PC
Production Companies
Produce and deliver the show end to end, from concept through load-out.
HW
Hardware Companies
Build the gear the whole industry runs on.
SW
Software Companies
Build the platforms and control systems running behind every show.
RH
Rental Houses
Stock and prep the gear that ships out for every show.
PM
Production Managers
Own the schedule, budget, and logistics that make the show possible.
PD
Project Designers
Design the system architecture and technical plan before a single cable gets pulled.
EIC
Engineers In Charge
Owns the technical plan for the show and calls the shots when something breaks live.
VE
Video Engineers
Keep every camera, switcher, and feed color-matched and running clean.
PROJ
Projectionist
Align, focus, and run the projectors so the image lands exactly where it should.
MS
Media Server Operators
Run the playback and content engine driving screens, LED, and video cues live.
GFX
Graphics Operators
Build and drive the on-screen graphics — lower thirds, stat boards, and show packages.
AE
Audio Engineers
Build and run the mix — from console gain structure to the monitor world.
FOH
FOH Engineers
Mix the room the audience actually hears — live, with no do-overs.
LD
Lighting Designers
Program and run the look of the show, cue to cue.
CL
Cloud & REMI Engineers
Route production remote, moving signal from the stage into cloud infrastructure.
CO
Camera Operators
Frame every shot the video director calls, live and on the fly.
TD
Technical Directors
Call the show and switch the picture in real time.
SC
Show Callers
Keep the whole production on cue and on time.
LB
Labor Brokers
Staff the crews that make load-in, show, and strike happen.
TC
Technicians
Keep the rig patched, powered, and running from load-in through strike.
SH
Stage Hands
Build, move, and strike the physical show — the muscle behind every load-in.
STG
Stage Managers
Call cues backstage and keep talent, crew, and schedule moving in sync.
TAL
Talent
The hosts and performers the whole production is built to support.
